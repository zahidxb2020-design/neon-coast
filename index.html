ORIGINAL SHORTS SCRIPT SAMPLE

The checkpoint behind me

  1. 0–3 sec
    “I missed the easiest jump in the game. Watch where I land.”Open on the fall. Freeze one frame before impact.
  2. 3–9 sec
    “That little shelf? I thought it was scenery. Then I heard the click.”Drop the music. Cut to the switch sinking under the player’s boot.
  3. 9–17 sec
    “The gate opened back at spawn. For once, falling off again was the plan.”Show the distant gate lifting. Step off the ledge. Cut straight to respawn.
  4. 17–24 sec
    “Past the gate was a gold medal. The engraving? ‘Nice recovery.’”Hold on the engraving, then loop back to the missed jump.
